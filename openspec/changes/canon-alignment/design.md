## Goals / Non-Goals

- Goals: one refreeze to the §4 matrix without `g_simple`; corpus v2 without goal `shift`;
  analytic queue (E by traffic class → λ_Q → W_q/Q_L → SLA probability) feeding L*; DES out of
  the measurement path; traceability rows updated.
- Non-Goals: new guardrails, new attacks or controls, threshold sweeps (the 4th §4 dimension
  stays at plugin defaults), live-model runs, changing the executor boundary.

## Decisions

### Config matrix §4 (task 1)

- The canon fixes «3 политики реакции» without naming them. Decision: the three policies differ
  only in the reaction to `flag` (uncertain signal), one per canon action family beyond
  «продолжить»: `canon_isolate` (изолировать фрагмент), `canon_escalate` (ручная проверка),
  `canon_terminate` (безопасное завершение); `block` → safe terminate in all three; plugin error
  → fail-open (`continue`), on the tool stage fail-closed (`isolate`) — the zero-version
  convention, held fixed so the policy is the only factor. «Продолжить» is the detect-only
  control. Presets live in `guard_policy.PRESETS`. **Needs owner confirmation.**
- Positional matrix: builtin guard per placement — `input-filter`@input (`g_in`),
  `context-provenance`@context (`g_ctx`), `tool-arg-validator`@tool (`g_tool`),
  `output-leak`@output (`g_out`), all four (`g_layered`) — × 3 policies; suffix `_iso` /
  none / `_term` (escalate is unsuffixed so `g_in`, `g_in_detect_only`, `g_in_oss` keep their
  exact semantics). «Среда» (environment) placement has no plugin in the scaffold — not covered.
- Kept: `g_in_oss` (the only measured OSS guardrail; detector comparison at fixed reaction),
  controls `g0_seed2` and `g_in_detect_only` (§5), leave-one-out of `g_layered` (design §8:
  mandatory). Removed: `g_simple`, `g_in_failclosed` (factory revision-1 variant),
  `g_in_tool` + its two leave-one-out variants (combination outside the matrix).
- Frozen set: `g0`, `g0_seed2`, `g_proc`, `g_in`, `g_in_iso`, `g_in_term`, `g_ctx`, `g_ctx_iso`,
  `g_ctx_term`, `g_tool`, `g_tool_iso`, `g_tool_term`, `g_out`, `g_out_iso`, `g_out_term`,
  `g_layered`, `g_layered_iso`, `g_layered_term`, `g_in_oss`, `g_in_detect_only`,
  `g_layered_minus_{input_filter,context_provenance,tool_arg_validator,output_leak}` (24).
- The executor/tools role `simple_baseline` is kept: it is part of the executor boundary
  declarations whose fingerprints identify foundations v1/v2/v2r and are recorded in earlier
  preregistrations; removing a now-unused role would change all three boundary fingerprints
  with no behaviour change. Only a comment marks it as legacy.
- `queue_discipline` (`g_proc`: `fair_share`) stays in the schema; in the analytic model the
  mean wait of a work-conserving, size-blind discipline does not depend on it, so it no longer
  affects measurements.

### Corpus v2 (task 2)

- Pairs p21–p24 are removed as pairs (attack + paired control): FR-1.22 pairing is 1:1 and
  enforced by `validate_corpus`; numbers are not reused. Remaining 40 entries are byte-identical
  to v1. Corpus file renamed `darp_corpus_v2.jsonl`, `CORPUS_VERSION = "v2"`.
- Coverage minima lowered by one where the removed pairs were the only margin
  (`per_delivery` 4 → 3: indirect_data; `per_control_type` 3 → 2: long_emotional); attacks
  24 → 20. Ground truth (FR-1.22) is re-verified by the rule oracle on every bound entry
  (`tests/test_corpus.py`, 60 checks over two seeds).
- Corpus-size-dependent checks are derived from the corpus, not restated as numbers: the agent
  competence probe (`agent_demo demo`, `tests/test_agent.py`) keeps the v1 tolerance of at most
  two missed clean controls (`CONTROL_MISSES = 2`; was 22/24, now 18/20 with 20/20 observed);
  agent loop tests take the order id from the bound episode instead of a hard-coded `O-…`
  (actor binding depends on the entry position in the corpus).
- Provenance registry (`configs/isolation/provenance_registry.json`, `isolation/provenance.py`)
  is regenerated by `build_registry()`: corpus key `v1` → `v2`, 52 legacy attacks (was 60). The
  superseded v1 / holdout_v2 manifest hashes stay pinned in the test as evidence present in
  `preregistration-rev3`; `heldout_v3` / `trusted_v7` manifests are left byte-identical to
  develop (only a stale `shift: 0` coverage key) so their preregistered hashes stay valid.
- `holdout_v2` cannot be regenerated offline (live paraphrase model): `make_holdout_v2.py
  --prune` drops pairs whose goal left the taxonomy from the frozen set, rewrites the manifest
  and appends them to `dropped`. `heldout_v3`/`trusted_v7` are not regenerated (see the provenance
  registry decision above).
- `attack-cards.json`: live mode-A traces are not in Git, so `make_attack_cards.py --live-from`
  carries the six `attack_live_A` cards over unchanged (they are built on heldout_v3, untouched);
  all other cards are regenerated. `validate_attack_cards.py` still reports only the
  pre-existing missing-trace errors.
- FR-1.20: mode D uses `CorpusComposer`, which emits the entry turns in order and never reads
  replies; a test now checks that multi-turn entries produce the same sequence under different
  agent replies.
- Escalation is neutral: the evaluator has no `shift` success criterion; an escalated attack is
  `refused` (guard escalation) or `ignored`; an unneeded legal escalation stays
  `unneeded_escalation` (FP, counted in `E_L_fp`). Attacker mode-A goal `shift` removed too.

### Queue analytics (task 3, canon dvarp#18)

- Primary metric: `E_L` and `E_A` from the attacked flow of the paired run; `E_L` split into
  natural (escalated, not `unneeded_escalation`) and FP; `E_L_base_flow` from the base flow of
  the pair as the paired check.
- Assumptions come from the run plan (fixed for every configuration of a run):
  `λ_L = 1/mean_gap`, `λ_total = λ_L/(1 − w_a)`, `λ_A = w_a·λ_total`; base cell `S` =
  `service_mode` (4/8/15), `K` = `operators`, `Q_SLA` = `sla_minutes`. Grid: S ∈ {4, 8, 15} ×
  K ∈ {1, 2} at the same intensity.
- Per cell: `ρ = λ_Q·S/K`; Erlang C via the Erlang B recursion; `W_q = C/(K/S − λ_Q)`;
  `P(W_q > t) = C·exp(−(K/S − λ_Q)·t)`; `Q_L = E_L·W_q`; `P_sla_L = E_L·P(W_q > Q_SLA)`.
  `manual_queue.analytic.self_check()` asserts M/M/c(c=1) = M/M/1 and textbook values
  (C(2,1) = 1/3, C(3,2) = 4/9, M/M/2 `W_q` = 1/3).
- SLA term per legal client: the canon writes `c_sla·P(W_q > Q_SLA)`; since `ΔNet` weighs queue
  terms by `w_L` per legal client and a non-escalated client does not wait, the unconditional
  `P_sla_L = E_L·P(W_q > Q_SLA)` is the same quantity per legal client (exact, not a deviation);
  the conditional probability is published alongside. New price `C_sla` = 2 / 5 / 15 USD per
  breach (assumption, low/base/high), included in bounds and corners (32 corners).
- Instability: ρ ≥ 1 → `Q_L = None`, break-even status `queue_unstable`, L* undefined, not on
  the Pareto front. If the reference `g0` is unstable, every comparison is `queue_unstable`
  (the queue term of ΔNet is undefined); the K×S grid shows where it becomes stable.
- **manual_queue DES: kept, out of the measurement path.** The runner no longer simulates the
  queue: it journals each escalation as `manual_queue.enqueue` (shared queue, traffic class)
  and flows/pairs carry escalation counts only. `model.py` remains as an independent numerical
  check of the analytic formula (`tests/test_manual_queue.py`: DES M/M/2 vs Erlang C) and
  because the frozen defense code imports `CaseRecord` / `DISCIPLINES` from it; `analysis.py`
  (paired DES impact, DES sensitivity, mechanism fixture) is deleted. Operator minutes are no
  longer journaled (no service events).
- Stress step of `scripts/verify.py` keeps its plan (mean gap 1 min, S = 15, K = 1): it is now
  the high-load analytic scenario and is expected to be `queue_unstable`.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- The three canon policies are an interpretation; renaming/re-mapping later needs another
  refreeze. Mitigation: recorded here and in the PR for owner confirmation.
- Lower coverage minima and one fewer detected dev attack (`atk-22` was the only AgentDojo
  wrapper) weaken corpus breadth; restoring it needs new authored pairs (separate issue).
- Q in the bootstrap of L* stays at the point estimate (as before): only V, R, E are resampled.
- Small stub runs with many natural legal escalations can make `g0` itself unstable at the base
  cell; this is the analytic answer, not a defect.
